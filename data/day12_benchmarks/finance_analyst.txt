Finance Analyst

We are seeking a Finance Analyst to support financial planning, analysis, and reporting.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related discipline.
- Strong analytical and numerical skills.
- Experience with financial modeling and budgeting.
- Knowledge of financial reporting and reconciliation.
- Ability to analyze financial data and prepare reports.
- Strong communication and attention to detail.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze financial data and business performance.
- Prepare budgets and financial models.
- Support forecasting and financial planning.
- Identify trends and cost-saving opportunities.
- Prepare reports for management.
