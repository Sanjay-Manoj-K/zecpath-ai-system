Data Scientist

We are seeking a Data Scientist to analyze data and build machine learning solutions.

Requirements:
- Strong knowledge of Python and data analysis.
- Experience with machine learning algorithms.
- Knowledge of statistics and data preprocessing.
- Experience with Pandas, NumPy, and visualization tools.
- Ability to build predictive models and communicate findings.
- Experience working with structured and unstructured datasets.

Responsibilities:
- Clean and preprocess datasets.
- Perform exploratory data analysis.
- Develop and evaluate machine learning models.
- Extract meaningful insights from data.
- Present analytical findings to technical and business teams.
