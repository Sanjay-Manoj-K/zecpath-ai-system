Legal Intern

We are looking for a Legal Intern to support attorneys with legal research, writing, and case preparation.

Requirements:
- Strong legal research and writing skills.
- Knowledge of legal principles and legal documentation.
- Ability to conduct case and statute research.
- Strong attention to detail.
- Excellent written and verbal communication.
- Ability to organize and analyze legal information.

Responsibilities:
- Conduct legal research.
- Prepare research memoranda and legal documents.
- Review case materials and summarize findings.
- Assist attorneys with case preparation.
- Organize legal information and documentation.
